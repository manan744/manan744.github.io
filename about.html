About Us

The Glassmic is a B2B glassware and gifting brand from Firozabad, India's City of Glass. Founded as a fresh, design-led venture, it has grown into a trusted partner for businesses across India.

Working with a strong network of skilled artisans, manufacturers and trade partners, we supply corporate gifting, wedding and festive gifting, and premium home décor — with customisation and reliable, on-time delivery.
